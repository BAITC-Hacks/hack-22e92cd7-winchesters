# Consolidated candidate list (deduplicated across the five research reports)
Cross-references: P = agent_methodology.md, I = agent_product.md, T = agent_technical.md, F = agent_fairness.md, L = agent_multilingual.md.

C01 Evidence Ledger — re-anchor on the client's 9 competencies × 3 BARS levels; every score decomposes into indicator-level tuples (competency, indicator, BARS anchor, verbatim quote verified by substring match, source, confidence); level derived by deterministic rules; "no_evidence" is a first-class state distinct from "weak". One object rendered as committee card, interviewer brief, candidate feedback. (P1, I1, T4, F6, L1)
C02 Two-stage blind scoring pipeline — extraction (quotes) → verification → BARS rating that sees only verified evidence, never demographics/raw essay; structured JSON-schema outputs; English rubric + native-language text; k independent samples, disagreement → "unresolved → human"; deterministic aggregation. (P5, T2, T4, T5, L1, L13)
C03 ATOLA mapper + "water"/specificity index — label spans as Action/Thinking/Outcome/Learnings/Application; flag missing components and dispositional-not-behavioral claims; output a checklist with quotes, never a deception score; flags become probes, never penalties. (P2, I15)
C04 Ipsative test ingestion + triangulation matrix — ingest the client's forced-choice results in their own bands; response-quality flags; claimed-vs-demonstrated matrix (test band × narrative evidence status); discrepancies → interviewer probes, never score changes. (P3, I2, T12)
C05 Interviewer pre-brief ("dig-deeper card") — one printable/phone page: 9 evidence states (no number), top ATOLA probes selected from the client's pre-approved probe bank, discrepancy alerts, water hotspots; score withheld to reduce anchoring. (P4, I7, T15)
C06 Post-interview note-to-BARS structurer — interviewer's typed/dictated notes mapped to ledger tuples with one-tap confirm; lists uncovered competencies; no live transcription. (I8)
C07 Candidate "Leadership Growth Map" — ledger-grounded development feedback in KZ/RU/EN: strengths quoting their own words, "not yet seen" competencies phrased as opportunity, one zero-budget next step each, re-application path; plan→render split with register (сіз) lints and native-speaker QA; committee release gate; competency 9 suppressed by default. (P10, I9, F7, T15, L11)
C08 Test-bank leak guard — n-gram/embedding similarity filter on all outward text (feedback, probes) against the 95 items; rewrite/drop and log. (I10)
C09 "Explain my result" + right to contest — candidate asks ≤3 grounded questions; "this does not reflect me" objection attached to the record; committee must record an outcome. (I11, F8)
C10 Remove demographic proxies from the score — delete school-type advantage table, adversity keyword decrement, GPA dimension, growth-delta; context (school, region, language, Foundation) shown to humans as flags with a live "Blind / Informed" toggle proving the AI score is identical. Hidden Gem redefined on behavior only. (P7, I4, T3, F11)
C11 Real fairness audit — group by region, school type, language/code-switch, Foundation/income proxy, gender + intersections; scoring-rate impact ratio (NYC LL144 method) with bootstrap CIs and 0.8 review line; band-stratified mean signed error once committee ratings exist; three visual states. (P6, F1, L10, T7)
C12 Counterfactual bias probe ("swap and rescore") — per-candidate button generating K variants (name, region, school, language via translation, disfluencies/code-switching) and re-scoring; tolerance = model's own re-score noise; cohort probe suite; fairness gate that blocks publishing a scorer version if probes fail. (F2, F3, F10, T6, P6)
C13 Cross-lingual consistency + style-invariance harness — 30-item parallel corpus (KZ/RU/EN + code-switched + rural-register variants); Krippendorff's alpha / level shift / rank correlation per language; translate-and-compare disagreement badge; CI regression test. (F4, L2, L3, L12, P14)
C14 Manner-of-speech firewall — normalize transcripts to neutral-register propositions before scoring, keep originals side by side; paired-transcript test asserts identical ledgers. Contested: methodology and multilingual agents prefer quotes-only scoring; translationese bias risk. (I5)
C15 Leadership Scenario Lab — transform the Feynman challenge into a 6–10-turn open-response simulation with AI teammates whose forks map to Teamwork, Values, Leadership abilities, Wounded leadership (+ optional teach-back for Intellect); voice/text KZ/RU/EN; scored into the ledger against BARS with quotes; "not observed" allowed; scenario editor for methodology owners; weight adjustable to zero; validated on Stage-2 data before weighting. (I6, P11, T14)
C16 Side-by-side Evidence Compare — 2–4 candidates × 9 competencies, one best quote per cell, BARS anchor in row header, "show only where they differ". (I12)
C17 Contrastive explanations — per competency, the minimal missing evidence to reach the next BARS level in the anchor's own words; one engine feeding committee tooltip, interviewer probe, and candidate feedback. (I14, F6)
C18 Calibration pack + disagreement radar + interviewer analytics — frame-of-reference anchor answers per competency × level in 3 languages with "trap" answers; 20-min in-app calibration; per-interviewer severity/leniency and drift; AI-vs-interviewer kappa; override reason codes feed calibration sessions. (P8, I13, F13)
C19 Decision Memo export + auto-filled Model Card + Impact Assessment — per-candidate PDF (levels, quotes, test bands, overrides with reasons, probe results, model/prompt hash, signatures); cohort memo for funder; model card and EU-AI-Act-Art.27-style impact assessment generated from live data. (I17, F12)
C20 Triage queues with the right to abstain — screen-out only by published deterministic rules (missing/invalid materials); "strong remote evidence" queue; "interview needed" queue sorted by information need, not score; abstention rate shown as health metric. (I3)
C21 Trajectory "then → now" timeline — dated initiatives with scope extracted from the candidate's own narrative; slope shown as context, never scored; replaces the demographic growth delta. (I19)
C22 Community evidence channel — optional nominated teacher/peer fills a 3-question structured form; third-party evidence tag, low adjustable weight; never penalize absence. High risk (forgery, unequal access, minors). (I20)
C23 Wounded / purpose-driven leadership as flags-only — indicator ledger (hard episode present, awareness link 0–3, concrete behavior change, uses experience to support others, mission→sustained action) with attention flags routed to interviewer; no level assigned by AI; never solicit trauma in the form; no feedback on competency 9 without committee review. (P9)
C24 Claim ledger / cross-source consistency — extract discrete claims (role, scale, outcome, duration) across essay, video, form, scenario; mark consistent/inconsistent/unsupported; inconsistencies → probes ("clarify", never "lie"). (P15, L7)
C25 Kill the "AI-text / authenticity score" — detectors have 50–100% false-positive rates on non-native writing and no Kazakh calibration; replace with C24 + C03; at most a neutral "verify authorship" interviewer hint. (L7, F15, T11, I-exclusions)
C26 Video pipeline: written version canonical, ASR for quotes only — form asks for a 150–300-word written version (any language) as the scoring input; ASR (Scribe v2, pinned; never Whisper for Kazakh) surfaces 3–6 timestamped quotes for the interviewer with per-word confidence highlighting; gate transcript-derived metrics on confidence; Stage 2 dual-ASR agreement (ISSAI Söyle in-country) and human-verified transcripts for borderline cases. (L8, L14, T11)
C27 Code-switch-aware language ID + bias-exclusion manifest — GlotLID + span-level LID with "mixed" as first-class; Latin-Kazakh transliteration; machine-readable excluded_features.yaml (language label, fillers, word length, speech rate, pauses, school type, region, name…) rendered on the dashboard with a unit test asserting none reach a scorer. (L4, L9)
C28 Stylometry v2 — lemma-level MATTR and sentence stats only, per-language empirical norms (versioned JSON), drop hapax/word-length/filler ratios; advisory percentile, never a score input. (L6)
C29 Engineering foundation — anthropic SDK ≥1.7 and current model IDs (Sonnet 4 is retired; live demo currently fails), AsyncAnthropic + semaphore, structured outputs, SQLite→Postgres with append-only model_runs/ratings/overrides/audit_log, rubric+prompt versioning with content hashes, JWT+argon2 RBAC (applicant/interviewer/committee/admin) on every router, CORS allowlist, PII v2 (pseudonyms, KZ/RU/EN NER redaction, Cyrillic phone/IIN regexes, all free-text fields), prompt-injection firewall via <document> tags + quote verification, Feynman session hardening, Batch API + 1h cached rubric, cost telemetry, no mock transcripts scored. (T1, T2, T8, T9, T10, T13, T14; audit items A1–A34)
C30 Evaluation harness v0/v1 + pre-registered validation plan — synthetic gold set (9×3×3 languages), counterfactual probes, repeat consistency, cross-lingual agreement, injection suite; Stage 2: QWK/ICC vs committee per competency, calibration tables, adverse impact by group, screening-safety check, ablations; one-command HTML report; model card page states where the AI is weak (expect Purpose-driven and Wounded) and abstains. (T6, T7, P12, I18)
C31 Red-team log + attack-success dashboard — versioned log of adversarial tests (fake authority, emotional framing, verbosity padding, socially desirable phrasing, name/region swaps, dialect injection) with attack success rate per scorer version. (F9)
C32 Funnel disparate-impact monitor — applied → complete → AI pre-score → invited → admitted, impact ratio per transition with the AI stage isolated. (F14)
C33 Mechanical combination with client-owned weights + append-only override ledger — composite = Σ weight × level from a signed, versioned rubric config labeled "owned by inVision U / Talent Craft"; bands not decimals; overrides at indicator/level with mandatory reason, never overwrite; model vs committee composite side by side. (P13, A13, A15)
C34 Honesty prime at application — "your examples will be discussed in the interview" warning (the one lever with evidence for reducing deceptive impression management). (P15)
C35 Per-language calibration of scores/thresholds — replace fixed 70/50 with within-declared-language percentile bands or Stage-2 learned offsets; show raw vs calibrated and its effect on impact ratios. (F5)

Excluded by consensus (do not vote for): facial/emotion analysis; voice-stress/prosody/fluency scoring; eye tracking; social-media scraping; demographic multipliers in score; AI-detection as penalty; live real-time Kazakh transcription as scoring input; inferring Foundation/family status from text; automatic quotas; admit/reject prediction without per-competency decomposition.
